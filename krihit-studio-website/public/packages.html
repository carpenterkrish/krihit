How to Book Your Wedding

Follow these steps to reserve your date with KRIHIT STUDIO.

01

Select Your Package

Choose the photography and film package that suits your wedding plans and budget.

02

Confirm Date & Details

Contact us on WhatsApp. We will confirm date availability, coverage, deliverables and any additional charges before payment.

03

Pay 50% Advance

Once the details are agreed, pay the 50% booking advance using the payment method confirmed by the studio.

04

Receive Your Booking Slip

After we verify the payment, KRIHIT STUDIO will issue a booking confirmation slip/receipt with your event and package details. Keep it safe—it is your written proof of confirmed booking.

Please remember: Your date is not confirmed just by selecting a package or sending a payment screenshot. Booking is final only after the studio verifies the advance and sends written confirmation/receipt. The remaining 50% is payable before final delivery, as agreed.
